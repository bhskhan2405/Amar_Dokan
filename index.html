Privacy Policy for Amar Dokan

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

At Amar Dokan, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how our mobile application handles your personal and business information.

Amar Dokan is a POS and Inventory management tool. We collect and store information that you provide voluntarily, such as:

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase. We implement industry-standard security measures provided by Firebase to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

The app may request permissions such as Camera (for barcode scanning) and Storage (for saving PDF reports). These are only used for the intended app functions.
