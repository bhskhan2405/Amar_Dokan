Last Updated: August 26, 2026

Amar Dokan ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our users. This includes:

  • Personal Information: Name, Email Address, and Phone Number provided during registration.
  • Business Data: Shop name, Shop address, and contact details.
  • Operational Data: Product details, inventory stock, sales transactions, and customer debt (Baki) records.
  • Staff Information: Name and login credentials for authorized staff access.

2. Device Permissions

To function correctly, the app requests access to:

  • Camera: Used for scanning product barcodes and capturing product/profile images.
  • Storage: Used to save and export PDF invoices and business reports.
  • Biometrics: Used for secure login via fingerprint or face ID. We do not store biometric data; it is handled securely by your device's operating system.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored and processed using Google Firebase. We use secure authentication and database rules to ensure that only you and your authorized staff can access your business information. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or business data with third parties. Your information is used strictly for the purpose of managing your shop and inventory within the Amar Dokan application.
